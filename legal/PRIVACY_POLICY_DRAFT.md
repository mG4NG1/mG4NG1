## Information collected
- Google account identifier, verified email and display name when signing in.
- Profile information you provide: trade, business name, biography, phone number and profile photo or logo.
- Messages, crew connections, notices, and safety reports.
- Approximate location **only when location permission is granted and the member chooses to share an area**; optional Radar visibility is controlled by the member. A grid-rounded area, not exact live GPS, is intended to be stored.
- Basic account, session and technical security records.
- Subscription entitlement and transaction status returned by Google Play / RevenueCat. ChopOut does not receive full payment-card details.
- Contact phone numbers may be sent to our backend only when a user actively chooses contact matching. Contact names are not required by the matching API.

## Uses
We use information to operate profiles, search, crew requests, messaging, noticeboards, Radar, subscriptions, security and customer support. Users can choose whether their approximate area is visible on Radar. We do not sell personal data.
