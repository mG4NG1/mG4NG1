# Request deletion of a ChopOut account — website content draft

To delete your ChopOut account, open **ChopOut → Profile → Delete account** and follow the confirmation steps.

If you cannot access the app, email **mamimo.support@gmail.com** from your account email with the subject **ChopOut account deletion request**. We will verify that you control the account before processing the request.

Deleting an account removes its profile, connection records, notices and associated personal information from the live service. Some data may need to be retained temporarily to comply with legal obligations or backup schedules; these details must be disclosed accurately after the hosting setup is finalised.

**Important:** Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately through **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**.
