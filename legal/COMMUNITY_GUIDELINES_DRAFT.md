# ChopOut Community Guidelines — DRAFT

ChopOut connects adults in the trades. Be professional, respectful and truthful.

**Not allowed:** harassment, bullying, threats, hate, scams, spam, impersonation, misleading qualifications, illegal content, unsolicited commercial promotions, stalking, sharing another member's private details or trying to track members through Radar.

**Safety:** Radar positions are approximate, may not be live and must never be treated as precise location. Don't upload another person's photo, contact details or worksite information without permission.

**Reporting and blocking:** Users must be able to report inappropriate notices, messages and accounts, and block other users. Reports may be reviewed and content removed or accounts restricted. The feature must be tested before public launch.
